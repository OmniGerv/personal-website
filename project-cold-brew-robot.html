Cold Brew Robot

electronics product design 3d printing

Overview

This project is a cold brew coffee dispenser that pulls pre made coffee from a large jug from the fridge and dispenses a portion. It features 4 liquid chanels (coffee, milk, simple syrup and chocolate syrup), to enable different recipes. It can make coffee with milk and sugar, mochas, and even chocolate milk!

Challenges & Solutions

The main challenge for this project was the user interface. I wanted something very clean, almost analog feeling. I ended up deciding to only use (ON)-OFF-(ON) switches (two way momentary contact) and some short loops of the tubing being visible by the user as feedback. To select a recipe, the user presses down on the various ingredients of that recipe to "load" the ingredients, and thus selecting the recipe. The user knows that the ingredients are sucessfully selected as the robot will pull the liquid into the tubes visible section on the front of the robot. With a valid recipe loaded, the user can press down on any of the switches with a loaded ingredient and the robot will dispense the liquids in the correct proportions. If the user wished to unload an ingredient, they can press up on the switch and the liquid will be pumped back into the jug. After a short period of inactivity, the robot will automatically pump all liquids back into the jugs to keep cold.

With 1 gallon of coffee and milk loaded, my partner and I stay well caffeinated for 2 weeks! I run warm soapy water through the system once a month to keep it clean.

Technical Details

The following are a list of the components used in this project:

  • ESP32 with over the air updates
  • 4 peristaltic pumps
  • 4 switches
