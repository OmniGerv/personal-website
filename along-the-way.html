Along The Way

This page is for little tips, tricks and anecdotes I've picked up or come up with along the way in doing what I do. Figured I'd write them down.

Life

Crack your eggs in the sink

Cracking eggs always leaves a bit of egg white on the cracking surface which I find tedious to clean up. I like cracking them against the inside of the sink to make cleanup easier the next time you do the dishes. Plus I find cracking on a surface rather than an edge reduces the chance of small bits of shell getting in my omelet.

Baking soda scrubbing

If you have a dish with stubborn stains, grimy bathtub or sink, baking soda makes an amazing mild abrasive to get rid of almost any unwanted residue. Make sure the surface or dish is dry before starting. Sprinkle a liberal amount of baking soda on the surface and a squirt of dish soap. You don't want to add any water at this stage as you are looking for a paste to form. Even a little water will dissolve the baking soda away. Use a rag or sponge to scrub.

This works great for getting rid of brown stains on your Pyrex pans. If you use this trick on stainless steel pans, wipe on a teaspoon of vinegar to leave it watermark free and looking new! Make sure you reseason cast iron cookware after using this method.

VHB

VHB stands for Very High Bond, and it's a double-sided tape made by 3M. This is not your grandpa's duct tape! If you need something to be virtually permanently attached, and you don't want to use fasteners, VHB is the way to go.

Recipes

Foccacia Bread

Ingredients:

  • 4 cups of all purpose flour (or bread flour if you have it)
  • 2 cups warm water
  • 2 tbsp active dry yeast
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp crushed rosemary (optional for flavor)

Combine water, yeast and sugar in a bowl and let sit for 10 minutes. Add flour, salt and rosemary to a large bowl and mix well. Add the yeast mixture and stir until a dough forms. Knead a few times. Let sit covered for 1 hr to let the dough rise.

Grease a glass 13x9 inch baking dish and place the dough in it. Use your fingers to coax it into the corners. Let rise again for 30 minutes - 1 hr.

Brush tops with olive oil and sprinkle with a little sea salt. Bake at 425 for 10 min. Then 350 for 10 min. Let cool.

Tip: I have made many different flavors of this bread. I like to add olives and sun dried tomatoes on the surface for a tuscan twist. Or apple chucks tossed in brown sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg and allspice. Experiment!

Sweet and Spicy Mustard

Ingredients:

  • 1/3 cup yellow mustard seeds
  • 1/3 cup brown mustard seeds
  • 2/3 cup apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp salt
  • A few twists of black pepper
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1/2 lemon juiced
  • 3 garlic cloves crushed

Combine all ingredients in a jar and stir to combine. Let sit on the counter for 3 days. Then, blend/crush mixture to desired consistency. Let sit in the fridge for 3 weeks to let the flavours mellow out. Flavours may continue to mellow even past the 3 week mark, if you are still finding it too strong.

Tip: If you like a REALLY strong mustard flavour, swap some of the apple cider vinegar for water. Try a 50/50 mix.

Cutting Board Conditioner

Ingredients:

  • 30g beeswax
  • 30g coconut oil
  • 30g mineral oil
  • A few drops of Vitamin E oil (optional to extend shelf life)

Combine all ingredients, except for the Vitamin E oil, in a jar and place in a pot with a few inches of water. Over medium heat, bring the water up to just below a boil and stir wax until fully melted. Set aside and wait a few minutes for the mixture to cool before adding the Vitamin E oil. After ading the Vitamin E oil, stir well and let the mixture solidify before using.

Nerd Stuff

Cos is close

A mnemonic to quickly remember whether to use cos() or sin() to find the projection of a vector. The vector length multiplied by the cosine of the angle gives the projection CLOSEST to the angle (the projection vector that forms the given angle). Thus, cos is close.
