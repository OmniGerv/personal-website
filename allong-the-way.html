Along The Way

This page is for little tips, tricks and anecdotes I've picked up or come up with along the way in doing what I do. Figured I'd share.

Cos is close

A mnemonic to quickly remember wether to use cos() or sin() to find the projection of a vector. The vector length multiplied by the cosine of the angle gives the projection CLOSEST to the angle (the projection vector that forms the given angle). Thus, cos is close.

Crack your eggs in the sink

Cracking eggs always leaves a bit of egg white on the cracking surface which I find tedious to clean up. I like cracking them in the sink to make clean up automatic the next time you do the dishes. Plus I find cracking on a surface rather than an edge reduces the chance of small bits of shell getting in my omelet.

Baking soda scrubbing

If you have a dish with stubbon stains, grimey bathtub or sink, baking soda makes an amazing mild abrasive to get rid of almost any unwanted residue. Make sure the surface or dish is dry before starting. Sprinkle a liberal amount of baking soda on the surface and a squirt of dishsoap. You dont want to add any water at this stage as you are looking for a paste to form. Even a little ater will disolve the baking soda away. Use a rag or sponge to scrub.

This works great for getting rid of brown stains on your pyrex pans. If you use this trick on stainless steel pans, wipe on a teaspoon of vinegar to leave it watermark free and looking new! Make sure you reseason cast iron cookware after using this method.
